Welcome

Small hostel. Big windows. The whole old city on your doorstep.

The Castle Star sits on the hillside just below Jabal al-Qal'a, where Amman began. Walk down to the Roman Theatre and the souks of Al-Balad, or up to the Temple of Hercules. Then come back for the view.

Inside you'll find handmade wooden bunks, painted doors and murals, a lounge full of plants, and a kitchen you can actually cook in. Plenty of guests end up extending their stay.

  • Walk everywhereCitadel, Roman Theatre and Downtown are all on foot.
  • Sunset from the terraceThe whole of Amman turns gold, then lights up.
  • A family that looks after youTours, taxis, directions, even tracking down lost luggage. Just ask the desk.

The terrace

The best seat in Amman costs a cup of tea.

The terrace looks down onto the 2nd-century Roman Theatre and across seven hills of white stone. Watch the sunset, hear the evening call to prayer echo across the valley, and swap travel stories with people from everywhere.

There's a barbecue for warm nights, and a fully equipped kitchen right beside it.

Questions

Before you arrive.

How do I get here from the airport?

We can arrange a car from Queen Alia International Airport for US$25 one way (up to 4 people). Just add it to your WhatsApp message. On a budget? Take the Sariyah Express bus to Tabarbour station, then a short taxi to us.

Can I pay by card?

Payment is in cash at the property. There are ATMs and money changers a few minutes' walk away in Downtown, and the desk can help with currency exchange.

Is there a women-only dorm?

Yes. The 6-bed female dorm has privacy curtains and personal lockers for every bed.

Do you serve breakfast?

Yes, for a small extra charge: local favourites with vegetarian and halal options, plus juice, pancakes, cheese and fruit. You're also welcome to cook in the shared kitchen.

Can you help with tours?

The tour desk arranges walking tours of old Amman and day trips around Jordan. Ask on WhatsApp before you arrive or at the desk.

I'm arriving late. Is that OK?

The front desk is open 24 hours. Check-in runs until midnight, so let us know your arrival time if you'll be later.

Is there parking?

Yes, free parking is available for guests.
